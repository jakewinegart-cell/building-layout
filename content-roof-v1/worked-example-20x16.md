## Takeaways for Jake
1. Measure the house eave height first. It decides whether a house-high shed is even possible.
2. The 4:12 gable tie-in fits under the house ridge with about 3 ft to spare. Two 16 ft valleys, closed-cut over ice & water.
3. Either way, plan on a **new bearing wall next to the house** (HUD add-on rule) rather than a ledger, unless the manufacturer approves.
4. The snow load must be confirmed for the exact site. At 70 psf the 2x10 @ 24 gable still passes; the 2x12 @ 16 shed does not.
